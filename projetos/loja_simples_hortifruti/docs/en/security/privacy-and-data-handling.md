## Privacy & Data Handling

The application adheres to **Privacy by Design** principles to ensure customer data protection.

## Privacy Principles

1. **No Invasive Tracking**: The application does not deploy third-party tracking cookies or behavioral advertising scripts.
2. **Transient Checkout Data**: Customer contact and shipping details are stored locally on the user's device solely to assist checkout completion.
3. **Direct Peer-to-Peer Transmission**: Order summaries are transmitted directly to the store owner's WhatsApp without intermediary storage servers.
4. **Data Protection Compliance**: Personal details are handled exclusively for fulfilling customer orders upon explicit user action.
